How this plan works

Strength, endurance and a strong core, without bulking up.

On one or two off days, do 30–40 minutes of easy jogging, cycling or brisk walking at a pace where you can still talk.

Warm up first

Every session starts with 10–13 minutes on the treadmill. Then do 1–2 lighter sets of the first lift before your working sets.

Rest between sets

  • Strength lifts: 2–3 minutes.
  • Supporting moves: 60–90 seconds.
  • Finishers: as little as possible.

Ticking a set starts the rest timer for you.

How to progress

  • Strength lifts: when every rep of every set is done with good form, add a little weight next time (2.5–5 kg for legs, 1–2.5 kg for upper body).
  • Conditioning: cut rest, add a round or add reps. Don’t go heavier.
  • Abs & core: hold longer, carry heavier, or slow the reps down.

Eating

Eat around maintenance calories with enough protein to recover. You’ll still get noticeably stronger and fitter without adding bulk.

Install it on your phone

  • iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the link in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • Always open it from the Home Screen icon. It works offline after the first time, so bad gym signal won’t stop it.

Keeping your progress

  • Your weights and history are saved on this phone only.
  • Tap Back up on the History tab every couple of weeks. Use Restore to bring everything back on a new phone.
  • Use Copy log to share on the History tab to send your numbers to your coach.
  • Tap the plan name at the top of the Workout tab to rename it.
